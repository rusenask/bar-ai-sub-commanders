# Lessons: legion-vs-random

Written by the strategist's debriefs after games; +confirmed / -contradicted by later games.

## mid

- #2 [mid] When the audit says 'N factories but only M construction turrets: scale one lab with turrets before adding labs', lower max_factories and raise construction_turret_rate/nanos_per_lab so income goes into build power and units instead of extra factories and turrets. (+1/-0)
- #3 [mid] On All That Simmers the enemy masses from the south and south-east - Intelligence's blind southern approach kept drawing defend requests, so put radar/pickets and a guard there early rather than letting raiders only scout it. (+1/-0)

## late

- #1 [late] At a push-phase with a huge army lead, keep turret_budget_scale honest: the audit flagged 7708 metal of turrets against a 2622 budget while we only had 2 labs with 4 construction turrets - scale the lab with construction turrets (nanos_per_lab) before adding more static defence. (+1/-0)
- #5 [late] When we clearly outweigh the enemy in a push phase, let armyStyle go to skirmish/auto and keep money in mobile units; static turret value far above budget starves the army that is supposed to convert the lead. (+1/-0)

## all

- #4 [all] Argue for keeping economy posture balanced through T2: delivering the T2 lab and converters without conserving let metal stay fully spent into a 67-minute win - do not drop to conserve just because storage is thin. (+1/-0)
